Usage Guidelines

Logo Files: All logo files are PDF/SVG vectors—do not rasterize or recolor.

Color Accuracy: Use only approved color values; do not substitute.

Teal Steel System: Use for premium interfaces, glass morphism effects, and sophisticated gradients. Perfect for headers, hero sections, and immersive experiences.

Clear Space: Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "M" around all logos.

Glass Effects: Combine backdrop-filter: blur() with teal steel colors for liquid metal aesthetic.
